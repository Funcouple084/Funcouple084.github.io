You're Not The Only One

The Confession Room is intended to become a place where people can read about experiences that they may otherwise never hear about.

Stories may lead to conversations, different perspectives and lessons that can help people understand themselves and others a little better.

Advice and comments should always be shared respectfully. Different people can experience the same situation very differently.

About The Confession Room

The Confession Room was created around a simple idea: everyone has a story.

Some stories are funny. Some are painful. Some are embarrassing. Some are inspiring. And some are things people have never felt comfortable saying out loud.

"Sometimes telling your story is the first step toward understanding it."

This is a space for honest experiences and respectful conversations — without judgement.
